Dr. Aris Thorne is a brilliant but reclusive chronophysicist who, in 2042, invented the "Chrono-Leap Engine." This device allows for instantaneous travel between two points in spacetime, but only for inanimate objects. Dr. Thorne's primary research goal is to safely transport living organisms. He lives in a secluded cabin in the Swiss Alps, powered entirely by geothermal energy. His cat is named Boson.
